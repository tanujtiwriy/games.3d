FaceOff Arena

How to play: fight with your face, no controller needed.

The Goal

Two players duel in front of one webcam. Drain your opponent's health to zero for a K.O., or have more health left when the 30-second round timer ends. Win two out of three rounds to win the match.

Your Moves

👊

Punch

Raise your eyebrows

A quick attack. Short cooldown between punches.

🛡️

Shield

Pout (lips forward)

Blocks incoming hits while your shield bar lasts.

🔥

Fireball

Open your mouth wide

A powerful ranged attack. Needs the SPECIAL bar to be full.

💨

Dodge

Tilt your head

Slip past an incoming hit or fireball completely.

Bars and Damage

Item How it works
Health Both players start each round at 100. A punch does 9 damage, a fireball does 25.
Shield bar Drains while you hold your shield and absorbs hits. When it hits empty your shield locks until it has recharged a little, so don't hold it forever.
SPECIAL bar Fills over time on its own. When it says SPECIAL READY, open your mouth to fire. Using it resets the bar.

Pro Moves

  • Perfect Block ✨: raise your shield (pout) just as a hit lands. You take no shield damage, the shield bar is refunded, and your opponent gets hit with a counter for 10 damage.
  • Combo 🔥: land hits back to back (within about 1.5 seconds). From the third hit onward each one deals bonus damage, up to +9. The combo counter appears on screen.
  • Rage Mode 😡: when your health drops below 30, your hits deal 30% more damage and your SPECIAL bar charges almost twice as fast. A comeback is always possible.
  • Changing arenas 🌈: each round has a different colour theme, so every round feels fresh (supported in Chrome, Edge and Firefox).

Match Rules

  • Rounds: best of three, 30 seconds each.
  • K.O.: health reaching zero ends the round at once.
  • Time up: whoever has more health wins the round. Equal health is a draw.
  • Positions: Player 1 stands on the left, Player 2 on the right.
  • Solo play: choose Mode: vs Bot on the setup screen. The Bot plays as Player 2.
  • After the match: scan the QR code on the winner screen to download your battle photo, or use Save card.

Before You Fight

  1. Open the game at localhost:3000 and allow camera access.
  2. Stand side by side and make sure both faces show up on the setup screen.
  3. Press Calibrate and keep a relaxed, neutral face for two seconds.
  4. Practice each move and watch the icons light up.
  5. Press Enter arena and have fun.
Good lighting makes face tracking much more accurate. Face the camera and keep your whole face in view.

Tips

  • Save your shield for fireballs, since they hurt the most.
  • Mouth wide open is a fireball, while a small pout is a shield. Keep them clearly different.
  • Don't spam punches. Use the gaps to shield or dodge.
  • Learn the timing of the Perfect Block, because it turns defence into offence.
  • If a move won't trigger, raise the Sensitivity slider or calibrate again.
